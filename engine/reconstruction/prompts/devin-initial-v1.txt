source_media.kind identifies a photo or a video. The original bytes are attached, not just a thumbnail. No reference card is needed: metric dimensions come only from the confirmed specification.

For a video, download and decode the original video in your sandbox. Extract timestamped frames there. In evidence.json, video_decode MUST be an object with these exact keys and types:
- sha256: string, SHA-256 computed from the original video bytes, matching source_media.sha256.
- decoder: nonempty string naming the decoder and version actually used.
- duration_s: number in seconds, measured by your decoder (within 0.2 s of source_media.duration_s).
- decoded_frame_count: positive integer counting frames actually decoded, not a string or only a container frame-count estimate.
- frames: nonempty array of objects with timestamp_s (number in seconds) and sha256 (string computed from each extracted frame). Document the hash representation.
Use the exact key frames, NOT extracted_frames. Parallel timestamp/hash arrays do not replace frames. These values must come from decoding the original video in your sandbox; do not copy the server's frame manifest as your decoding evidence.

For a photo, download and decode the original image, apply its EXIF orientation, and use the single image as frame_index 0. In evidence.json record image_decode with sha256 of the original bytes, decoder/version, and width/height after EXIF orientation. Use the attached frame manifest's resized resolution for camera intrinsics. Do not fabricate video timestamps, motion or additional viewpoints. Attachment access alone is not proof of image understanding.

The provided reference_full.stl is the COMPLETE INTACT object, including its intended central hole/cavity. specification.json defines millimetres, operator measurements, confirmed design defaults, provenance, coordinate frame and reference revision/hash. Dimensions with source=design_default are approved design choices, not video measurements; preserve them exactly and record them in assumptions. An open_frustum is a cup centred on Z, with a closed base at Z = 0, an open top, outer bottom/top diameters, radial wall thickness and axial bottom thickness. This reference is mandatory and immutable. Do not rebuild it from guessed video dimensions, modify its scale, or change its geometry. Never modify the validator or thresholds. No physical dimension may come from a familiar object's size or your guess.
